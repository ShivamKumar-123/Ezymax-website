Every broker eventually asks the same question: should we keep renting a white-label platform or build our own? The honest answer depends on your stage, budget and how much of your edge comes from the platform itself.

## What a white label gives you

- Launch in weeks with a proven platform
- Predictable monthly cost with hosting, updates and support included
- Branding across platform, portal, apps and emails

## What building your own gives you

- Full control of features, roadmap and data
- Lower per-client cost at very large scale
- A product you own, with the engineering responsibility that comes with it

## The hidden costs on both sides

- White label: feature requests wait for the vendor's roadmap
- Own platform: hiring, security, infrastructure and 24/7 on-call

## A practical decision framework

- Under a certain client count, white label almost always wins
- Build custom modules on top of a white label before replacing it
- When a custom platform makes sense, and how to phase the migration

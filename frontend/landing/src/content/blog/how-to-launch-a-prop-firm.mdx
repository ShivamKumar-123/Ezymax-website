Prop firms grow fast because the model is simple: sell evaluations, fund the traders who pass, share the profits. The firms that last are the ones whose technology automates evaluation, risk and payouts before volume arrives.

## Designing your challenge

- One-step, two-step and instant funding models
- Profit targets, daily loss, max drawdown and minimum trading days
- Pricing challenges and refund policies

## Automating rule checks

- Why manual monitoring breaks after the first few hundred accounts
- Real-time rule enforcement and instant account creation after payment

## Managing funded traders

- Dashboards with live objectives and progress
- Scaling plans, profit splits and automated payouts

## Protecting the firm

- Detecting copy-pasted and toxic strategies
- Risk team tools and clear escalation paths

## The technology checklist

- Challenge builder, trader dashboard, payment gateway, website and checkout
- CRM integration and reporting
